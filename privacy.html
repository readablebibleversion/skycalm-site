Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains what information the SkyCalm: Fear of Flying app (“SkyCalm”) collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. SkyCalm is made by Calming Audio LLC (“we”, “us”).

The short version: your journal, flight plans and progress stay on your device. An account is optional. If you create one, we store your name and email so you can sign in. We don't show ads, sell your data or use tracking or analytics tools.

Information stored only on your device

These are saved on your iPhone or iPad and are never sent to us:

  • Journal check-ins: how you feel, when, what helped, and any notes you write
  • Flight plans you add, such as departure time and flight length
  • Your progress figures, such as breathing minutes
  • Downloaded sounds and meditations, and your app settings

Deleting the app removes this information. It may be included in your device's own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple's privacy policy covers.

If you create an account

You can use SkyCalm without an account. If you choose to create one, we collect:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address. If you use Sign in with Apple, this may be a private relay address from Apple.
  • Whether you agreed to receive emails from us
  • The date your account was created

We use this to let you sign in and, only if you agreed, to send occasional emails about SkyCalm. You can unsubscribe at any time. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We never see or store your password.

Purchases

SkyCalm Premium subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. We don't receive your payment details. The app checks with Apple on your device to confirm whether you have an active subscription.

Notifications

If you add a flight and allow notifications, SkyCalm schedules check-in reminders on your device. These are created by the app itself, not sent from our servers, and we don't collect a notification token.

Audio streaming

Sounds and meditations are streamed or downloaded from Google Cloud Storage. Like any web server, it receives your device's IP address to deliver the file and may keep standard technical logs. We don't use these to identify you.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run SkyCalm:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) stores account information.
  • Google Cloud Storage hosts the audio files.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, purchases and subscriptions.

These providers process information on our behalf under their own privacy and security terms. We don't sell or rent your personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep account information for as long as you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app under About → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile and sign-in account from our systems. If you used Sign in with Apple, SkyCalm also revokes the app's access to your Apple ID. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, and to object to or limit how we use it. To make a request, email support@skycalm.net. We'll respond within 30 days.

Children

SkyCalm isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Information sent between the app and our providers is encrypted in transit. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect and who can access it.

Health information

Journal entries about how you feel stay on your device and are never sent to us. SkyCalm is a wellness app, not a medical device, and it doesn't provide medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.
